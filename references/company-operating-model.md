# Company Operating Model

Use this reference when framing Patent Capital OS as an enterprise-grade patent business system.

## Reference Mental Models

| Source archetype | Operating question | Patent Capital OS translation |
|---|---|---|
| Patent prosecution firm | Can this be drafted, filed, prosecuted, and defended cleanly? | Counsel review, claim quality, formalities, office-action readiness |
| Enterprise IP department | Does this protect a product, roadmap, standard, or strategic option? | Portfolio tags, family strategy, maintenance logic |
| Investment bank diligence team | Is this asset transaction-ready and risk-disclosed? | IP data room, ownership evidence, chain of title, risk memo |
| Venture investor | Does this increase moat, financing story, or market power? | IP investment memo, defensibility, why-now, competitive map |
| IP management platform | Can this be tracked without missed deadlines or bad data? | Docketing, fee events, receipts, audit log, version hashes |
| Litigation and invalidity team | How would a competitor attack or design around this? | Red-team review, fallback ladder, proof map |
| Standards and licensing team | Can this support SEP, FRAND, licensing, or patent-pool leverage? | Standards mapping, essentiality flags, licensing packet |

## Enterprise Case Lifecycle

1. Capture
   - Source invention from R&D, product, experiments, code, suppliers, customer failures, standards work, or competitor teardown.
   - Preserve invention evidence and disclosure dates.

2. Triage
   - Score strategic relevance, technical novelty, likely patentability, filing urgency, budget, and disclosure risk.
   - Decide patent, trade secret, defensive publication, or no action.

3. Diligence
   - Search patents, papers, standards, products, and public disclosures.
   - Build a closest-prior-art matrix.

4. Asset design
   - Convert technical contribution into claim architecture.
   - Design independent claim, fallback dependent claims, variants, and divisional/PCT space.

5. Legal and formalities
   - Confirm inventor, applicant, ownership, agency, signatures, secrecy review, and fees.
   - Lock final version hashes.

6. Filing operations
   - Generate official package, validate format/XML, submit through authorized channel, capture receipt.

7. Prosecution and maintenance
   - Docket deadlines, office actions, fees, divisional opportunities, continuation/PCT windows.

8. Portfolio monetization
   - Track product coverage, competitor coverage, standards relevance, licensing, assertion risk, abandon/maintain decisions.

## Decision Scorecard

Score each case 1-5:

- Product coverage.
- Competitive blocking power.
- Technical distinctiveness.
- Evidence quality.
- Patentability risk.
- Filing urgency.
- International relevance.
- Licensing or SEP relevance.
- Litigation defensibility.
- Maintenance cost fit.

Recommended actions:

- 38-50: core asset; file with full red-team and portfolio plan.
- 28-37: file if budget permits; use narrow fallback ladder.
- 18-27: consider defensive publication, trade secret, or limited filing.
- Below 18: hold unless deadline or business sponsor overrides.

## Board-Level Outputs

When asked for executive reporting, produce:

- IP investment memo.
- Portfolio heat map.
- Filing pipeline.
- High-risk legal gates.
- Top competitor prior art.
- Upcoming deadlines.
- Budget and maintenance view.
- Licensing/SEP opportunities.
