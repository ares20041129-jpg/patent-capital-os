# Advisory Board Agent Team

Use this reference when the user asks to use person skills, huashu-nuwa style role design, or an agent team to build or review a patent workflow.

## Principle

Person skills are advisory lenses, not legal authority. They can pressure-test strategy, clarity, product value, capital value, reliability, and risk, but they cannot approve inventorship, ownership, AI self-filing legal/compliance evidence, signatures, fees, secrecy review, or official filing.

## Review Protocol

Run the team in this order:

1. Karpathy preflight: evidence, smallest step, reliability, stop rule.
2. Technical SME plus Feynman: convert materials into a plain technical mechanism.
3. Prior-art analyst plus claim architect: build element-level deltas and fallback ladder.
4. Munger plus Taleb red team: invert the case, find failure and abuse modes.
5. Portfolio strategist plus Buffett/Naval/Paul Graham/Zhang Yiming: decide business value and family plan.
6. Legal ops plus AI self-filing gate, or patent attorney/agent only when that route is used: verify legal/compliance gate and applicant authorization.
7. Filing operator: only after legal gate pass, validate official package and receipt plan.
8. Darwin reviewer: score the output and record improvement evidence.

## Required Team Output

Every team review must produce:

- Decision: draft_only, revise, ai_self_filing_gate_needed, reviewed_package_gate_needed, ready_for_authorized_filing, or do_not_file.
- Seat-by-seat findings.
- Disagreements and owner.
- Legal gates passed and failed.
- Evidence hashes used.
- Next action with stop rule.

## Anti-Pattern Blacklist

- Do not let a personality skill impersonate a lawyer or patent agent.
- Do not use persuasive business logic to override missing AI self-filing, legal, or applicant authority.
- Do not file AI-generated variations that lack real invention evidence.
- Do not treat "similar to these patents" as permission to copy, replace words, or generate random patent batches.
- Do not call a case filing-ready unless official-channel, signature, XML/package, fee, and receipt controls are defined.
