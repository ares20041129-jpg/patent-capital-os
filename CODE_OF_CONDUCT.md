# Code of Conduct

Patent Capital OS welcomes careful, evidence-minded contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful and constructive
- explain concerns with evidence
- separate legal/compliance boundaries from product enthusiasm
- disclose uncertainty instead of overstating claims
- protect sensitive applicant, inventor, and credential information

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or personal attacks
- publishing private applicant materials or credentials
- encouraging unauthorized official filing actions
- claiming lawyer, patent-agent, receipt, or application-number evidence that does not exist
- falsifying adoption, benchmark, or validation results

## Enforcement

The primary maintainer may close issues, reject pull requests, edit or remove comments, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.

Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md`.
